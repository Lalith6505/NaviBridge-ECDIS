PRIVACY & MARITIME INTEGRITY

Privacy Policy

NaviBridge ECDIS enforces uncompromising maritime privacy and zero telemetry collection.

1. Offline Edge Operation

NaviBridge ECDIS does not transmit, synchronize, or back up your vessel coordinates, AIS tracks, route plans, or navigation logs to any cloud service or third party. All calculations run strictly on your local device.

2. Local Network Usage

Network permissions are utilized exclusively to bind a local UDP socket on your private shipboard Wi-Fi or bridge router (e.g. UDP port 10110) to receive broadcast NMEA 0183 or NMEA 2000 sentences from onboard marine electronics. No data leaves your local network.

3. Zero Analytics & Zero Ads

The application contains no commercial tracking frameworks, third-party advertising SDKs, or analytics trackers.

4. Local Data Storage

Custom chart caches, waypoint coordinates, and passage plans are stored in private sandboxed device memory, under the full control of the user.

5. Contact Information

For questions or support regarding NaviBridge ECDIS, please contact the developer via the official repository portal.
