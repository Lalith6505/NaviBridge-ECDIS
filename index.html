NaviBridge ECDIS

Enterprise-grade Electronic Chart Display and Information System with AIS anti-collision and shipboard NMEA gateway integration.

100% Offline Bridge Architecture

NaviBridge ECDIS operates fully offline at sea without requiring cloud servers, internet connectivity, or external tracking. All vector calculations, CPA prediction, and passage safety validation remain strictly local.

Vector Chart Engine

Real-time vector rendering of coastlines, depth contours, TSS lanes, lighthouses with light arcs, and navigational aids.

AIS / ARPA Anti-Collision

Continuous CPA and TCPA calculations across all tracked targets with visual and acoustic collision warning alarms.

Dynamic Safety Depth Contours

Configurable draft depth safety zones that highlight shallow water hazards and prevent grounding along your planned track.

Conning Display HUD

Real-time bridge conning telemetry providing True Heading, COG, SOG, Depth Under Keel, and Apparent/True Wind.

NMEA 0183 & N2K Gateway

Listens on local Wi-Fi UDP port 10110 to decode $GPRMC, $AIVDM, $SDDBT, $WIMWV, and $HEHDT sentences in real time.

Passage Planning & Waypoints

Full route schedule manager with leg course vectors, cross-track error (XTE) validation, and automated safety clearance checks.
